Free Online Typing Master - Learn English Typing & Take Typing Tests

PSDC Typing Master is a free typing tutor made by PAFWA Skills Development Centre (PSDC), NRK for DIT students and beginners. Learn touch typing step by step - home row, top row, bottom row, numbers, common words and full sentences - with an on-screen keyboard, finger guide, live WPM and accuracy. Typing seekhna ab asaan hai: bilkul free, bina login ke.

What you can do on this website

  • Typing lessons for beginners with finger-position hints and printable progress reports.
  • Typing speed tests (1-10 minutes) to measure your WPM and accuracy.
  • Typing games: Sky Shooter, Bubble Pop and Turbo Typing Racer to practise keys while having fun.
  • Online typing competition: challenge classmates 1 vs 1, in 2 vs 2 teams, or race everyone in a car race with Easy, Medium and Hard levels.

Frequently asked questions

Is PSDC Typing Master free?

Yes. All lessons, tests, games and the online arena are completely free to use.

How can I improve my typing speed (WPM)?

Keep your fingers on the home row, look at the screen and not the keys, focus on accuracy first and practise for 15-20 minutes every day. Speed follows accuracy.

Typing seekhne ke liye kitna time lagta hai?

Rozana 20-30 minute practice se aam taur par 4 se 8 hafton me achi speed aa jati hai.

How does the online typing competition work?

Open the Online Arena tab, enter your name, pick a game style (simple typing or car race), format (1v1, 2v2 or free for all) and level, then invite online students. When the race ends you can download a result image and share it on social media.
