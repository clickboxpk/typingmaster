TYPING BUBBLE SHOOTER

TYPE FAST. AIM SMART. POP BUBBLES!
Typing gameplay needs a physical keyboard. On a phone or tablet, connect one for the full experience.

How to play

  1. Look at the words inside the bubbles.
  2. Choose any visible word.
  3. Type that word using your keyboard.
  4. Correctly typed words destroy bubbles.
  5. Destroy bubbles before they reach the bottom.
  6. Earn points and complete levels.
  7. Improve your typing speed and accuracy.
